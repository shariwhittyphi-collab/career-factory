ND IN-DEMAND CAREER ASSESSMENTS - BUILD KIT
Updated: September 29, 2026

WHAT THIS IS
171 interactive HTML career assessments covering the entire North Dakota
In-Demand Occupations list, in 11 areas:
  Healthcare 30 | Skilled Trades 31 | Business and Finance 23 | Tech 18
  Architecture and Engineering 16 | Education 15 | Management 14
  Social Services 8 | Science 7 | Transportation 5 | Arts, Legal and Sales 4

TWO DELIVERY FORMATS
  1. Hub  - one self-contained .html per area. Assessments are base64 inside
           ASSESSMENTS_DATA and open in an iframe.
  2. Web  - a folder per area: index.html plus one page per career.
           Plus a landing index that links all 11 areas.

HOW TO BUILD
  python3 bk/tech/build.py <which>     # which = tech|health|health2|health3|
                                       # trades|trades2|it2|soc|trans|fin|eng|
                                       # edu|mgmt|sci|als|back
  python3 buildall.py <cat>            # cat = health|trades|tech|soc|trans|fin|
                                       #       eng|edu|mgmt|sci|als
  python3 combine.py                   # builds the full site/ folder

PIPELINE FOR ANY NEW ASSESSMENT - run these passes in this order:
  1. bk/hub/scrub.py        scrub_text(s)      removes DVR/VR wording
  2. bk/hub/a11y.py         add_a11y(s)        skip link, text size, dark toggle
  3. bk/hub/tools.py        add_tools(s)       per-section Listen + Print this part
  4. bk/hub/reportlisten.py add_report_listen(s)  Listen to my results
  5. bk/hub/darkfix.py      add_darkfix(s) + add_printfix(s)
  6. bk/hub/printfix2.py    add_printfix2(s, filename)   <-- NEW Sept 29
  7. bk/hub/sources.py      add_sources(s, name, soc)    <-- NEW Sept 29
  then build the hub. Every pass is idempotent - safe to re-run.

  For hub SHELL templates (*_tpl.html) only:
  bk/hub/hubprintfix.py     add_hubprintfix(s)           <-- NEW Sept 29

WHAT printfix2 AND hubprintfix DO (added Sept 29, 2026)
  Problem found in testing: Ctrl+P did not reliably print the report.
  Three different print behaviours existed across the templates, and in the
  hub the iframe clipped the report to a single page.

  printfix2 (per assessment page):
    - report generated  -> printing prints the report only, on every template
    - report not yet generated -> printing prints the page you are reading,
      never a blank sheet
    - forces white background and black text in print, dark mode included
    - adds a print-only heading at the top of the report:
      "<Career> - Career Assessment Report" plus the date
    - names the career in the on-screen report heading where it was anonymous
      (RN, CNA and LPN said only "Your Assessment Report")

  hubprintfix (hub shell):
    - hides the hub menu and viewer chrome when printing
    - reflows the iframe to the printed page width, measures the report at
      that width, and expands the frame so nothing is cut off

  Verified: all 171 careers, standalone and inside the hub, light and dark.
  Every printed report is 2-3 pages, carries the career-name heading, and
  ends where the report ends.

WHAT sources.py DOES (added Sept 29, 2026)
  Adds a "Sources & Learn More" block so people can see where the information
  came from. Four rows: four occupation-specific O*NET links and a general
  RUReady career-planning link:
    Career information        -> onetonline.org/link/summary/<soc>
    Pay and employment data   -> .../localwages/<soc>?st=ND   (BLS OEWS May 2025)
                                 .../localtrends/<soc>?st=ND  (Projections
                                 Central, 2024-2034)
    Education and training    -> .../localtraining/<soc>?st=ND
                                 real ND colleges and programs for that job,
                                 from the U.S. Dept of Labor using IPEDS data
    More career exploration   -> secure.ruready.nd.gov/Career_Planning/_default.aspx

  It is a standing block at the bottom of the page, NOT a numbered section.
  Three different section-navigation schemes exist across the templates, so a
  twelfth step would mean editing three sets of counters. The block carries its
  own Listen and Print this part buttons and prints together with the report.

  Occupation codes live in careers_soc.json, keyed by output filename.
  All 171 were checked against O*NET; GIS Technologist had been pointed at the
  generic "Computer Occupations, All Other" catch-all and was corrected to
  15-1299.02.

HARD RULES - DO NOT BREAK THESE
  * Never permanently delete anything. Recycle Bin only, and only with Shari's OK.
  * No "DVR" or "VR" anywhere in an assessment. Use "career counselor".
  * Every career needs at least 2 embedded images before it goes into a hub.
  * No text, letters, numbers or logos inside generated images.
  * Keep all the information. Do not cut content for length.
  * Spell her name Shari, not Sheri.
  * People in images: fictional adults, a real mix of backgrounds including
    Native American plus white, Black and Middle Eastern, varied ages and
    genders, ordinary work clothes doing ordinary work. No regalia, no costume,
    no cultural signalling. Spread across the batch.
  * FUNDING: financial aid, scholarships, grants and employer help come FIRST.
    VR is left out entirely. WIOA may be named but is never the first source.
    State no eligibility rules on client-facing pages.
  * bls.gov is blocked. Never fetch it and never work around the block.
    Wage and outlook data comes from O*NET OnLine localwages / localtrends.
