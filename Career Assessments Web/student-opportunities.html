RURAL COMMUNITIES. REAL POSSIBILITIES.

Try something near home.

You don’t have to pick a career to see what the work is like. Visit a workplace. Shadow someone. Try a healthcare camp. Ask about a student job. There are people doing interesting work in rural North Dakota.

Employer and program information checked October 4, 2026. These are places to explore, not promises of a place or a job. Openings change. Ask the employer or program about dates, age requirements, pay or costs, transport, school arrangements, and support.

While you’re still in school

A job shadow means observing someone at work. A co-op, work study, or internship gives you supervised work experience, sometimes arranged through your school.

STUDENT CO-OP · JOB SHADOW

Gooseneck Implement

Co-op: age 16 or older. The current student listing describes short-term work during a semester or school year, with projects, training, and time exploring sales, parts, administration, and service. Gooseneck also has a technician job-shadow inquiry form.

Where to ask: Co-op listings were found in Bowman, Elgin, Rugby, Velva, Minot, and Williston, ND, plus Lemmon, SD. Other Gooseneck towns include Beach, Harvey, Kenmare, Mohall, and Stanley; ask about a visit or future placement there.

TOUR · JOB SHADOW · PAID HIGH SCHOOL INTERNSHIP

RDO Equipment Co.

Paid high school internship: age 16 or older. RDO offers store tours, job shadows lasting 3–10 hours, and paid high school internships. Students can see service, parts, technology, sales, and administration. Internships are often coordinated with a school’s work-based learning program.

Where to ask: Lisbon and Hazen are two rural ND stores. A store location does not mean it has an open internship; ask what is available nearby.

Explore RDO’s high school programs

PART-TIME STUDENT EXPERIENCE

Titan Machinery — EDGE

Age 16 or older and still pursuing a diploma or GED. EDGE lets students explore dealership work with a mentor. The program runs at least 12 weeks, with at least four hours of part-time work a week. Titan accepts applications throughout the year. Students who have already completed high school or a GED are directed to other programs.

Where to ask: Titan has dealerships in LaMoure, Lidgerwood, Lisbon, and Northwood, among other ND communities. Confirm a local EDGE placement and ask about pay and scheduling.

Read about EDGE and how to apply · Find a Titan dealership

HIGH SCHOOL WORK STUDY

Butler Machinery

High school students, age 16 or older. Butler’s work-study program pairs students with a service-technician mentor. Students get hands-on exposure to agriculture equipment, diesel technology, and different departments before deciding on further training.

Where to ask: Butler serves ND communities including Jamestown and Dickinson. Its program page provides a North Dakota application route. Ask which nearby locations currently have placements, what the schedule is, and whether the placement is paid.

HIGH SCHOOL WORK · ASK ABOUT FUTURE OPENINGS

CHS — Elgin

CHS previously advertised an “Intern High School” position in Elgin. That listing is no longer on its current internship board, so this is a place to ask about future student work, rather than a confirmed opening.

Ask whether a high school experience is available near you, which ages can take part, and how work could fit your school schedule.

HEALTHCARE EXPLORATION · CAMP

UND Center for Rural Health — Scrubs

Explore healthcare before choosing a program. One-day Scrubs camps bring students together with local healthcare professionals in rural communities. These are career exploration experiences, not paid employment internships.

Scrubs Academy II is a four-day, three-night camp based in Minot for North Dakota students who have completed grade 9, 10, or 11. Academy I at UND serves students who have completed grade 6, 7, or 8. Ask about the next dates, registration, costs, and available support.

Planning ahead: work and training after high school

These routes involve further schooling or program admission. You can ask about them now, even if you are still exploring.

PAID WORK + COLLEGE TRAINING

Gooseneck — Virtual Ag Technician

Gooseneck combines full-time dealership employment with an online associate degree through Arkansas State University–Beebe. The current listing describes one day of coursework and four days of hands-on work each week, with August 2027 classes. School admission and employer requirements apply; this is a different route from the high school co-op.

Virtual Ag Technician listings were found at 13 locations, including Beach, Bowman, Elgin, Harvey, Kenmare, Mohall, Rugby, Stanley, and Velva. Ask about your location, the start date, tuition-help terms, and any relocation after graduation.

Read the current Dickinson Virtual Ag listing · Explore the program

COLLEGE SPONSORSHIP + PAID INTERNSHIP

Plains Ag

Plains Ag sponsors students in the NDSCS Case IH Diesel Technology program and offers paid dealership internships, tuition help, and tool support. The published route requires a diploma or GED and age 18 by the first internship, along with school and sponsorship requirements.

Where to ask: Beach, Bowman, Hettinger, Stanley, and Garrison are among its ND communities. Its inquiry form also has a job-shadow option; ask whether a visit is available before choosing a program.

Read Plains Ag’s training route and send your own inquiry

Planning ahead: internships while studying

Some internships require enrollment in a college or technical program. Others need an eligibility check before you apply. Learning about them now can help you see what is possible.

PAID AGRICULTURE INTERNSHIP · DEGREE STUDENTS

CHS — Drake

Summer 2027 agronomy internship: advertised pay $15–$21 an hour. The Drake listing requires current enrollment in Ag Business, Crop Science, or another agriculture-related degree program. Work includes crop scouting, inventory, customer service, and learning about equipment and agronomy operations.

The internship may begin in spring 2027, depending on business needs and student availability. Check the current listing for requirements and application details.

Read the Drake agronomy internship · Look for other CHS internships near you

PAID INTERNSHIP · CHECK SCHOOL AND AGE REQUIREMENTS

Agtegra Cooperative

Agtegra First internships offer paid work, flexible start and end dates around school, and a $750 scholarship upon completion. Areas include crop scouting, precision agriculture, agronomy and grain operations, IT, accounting, and more.

Where to ask: Agtegra has locations in Oakes, Berlin, Eldridge, Hague, and Strasburg, among other communities. Its program overview does not specify high school eligibility. Ask which ages and school programs qualify and whether a placement is available near you; a local facility does not guarantee an internship.

Explore Agtegra First internships · Find an Agtegra location

PAID ENERGY INTERNSHIP · DEGREE STUDENTS

Basin Electric Power Cooperative

Basin Electric offers paid internships for students working toward a degree. Placements may be at its Bismarck headquarters or a plant facility. Summer positions are generally full-time; some opportunities may let students work part-time while attending school.

It also works with Bismarck State College’s Power Plant Technology and Process Plant Technology programs on a paid two-week plant internship that is part of the curriculum. Ask about eligible programs, current locations, and the requirements for each role.

One question is enough to start.

You could ask: “I’m a student near your location. Could I visit, shadow someone, or learn about student experiences in an area that interests me?”

A school counselor, teacher, family member, or someone you trust can help you ask and plan the trip. Talk about the schedule, transport, school credit, supervision, and any accommodations that help you take part.

For training costs, start with financial aid, scholarships, grants, and employer help. Ask about the full costs and any work commitment before enrolling.
