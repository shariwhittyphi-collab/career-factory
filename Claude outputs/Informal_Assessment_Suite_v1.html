Four informal checks in one file

Pick a check from the list. It opens right here. The client can fill it in on screen, or you can read it with them. Each takes about 10 minutes.

Work Readiness Skills Check

Who: Adults and transition students getting ready to look for work, start a job or go back to work.
What: 30 statements about everyday skills in six areas: communication, attitude, teamwork, networking, problem solving and professionalism. The report lists strengths and the skills to work on, with an idea for each.
When: Before a job search or job placement, before a work experience or internship, or after a job ended over how the work went.

School and Training Readiness Check

Who: Anyone thinking about college, a certificate program or other job training.
What: 32 statements about the plan, study skills, time, money, support people, computer skills, self-care and using school help. The report gives a planning list with a next step for each item.
When: Before applying or enrolling, while building a training plan, or a few weeks before classes start.

Barriers to Work and School Checklist

Who: Any client.
What: 35 things that can get in the way, such as transportation, child care, housing, money and benefits, health, background, papers and skills, confidence and school. The client marks how big each one is and where they want help. The report turns that into a plan table.
When: At intake, when building or updating the plan, before a job start, or any time the client is stuck or life changes.

Free-Time Interests

Who: People who are not sure what work they would like, have little work history, or find long word-heavy lists hard. It works well for transition students.
What: 48 everyday activities with pictures, linked to six kinds of work, with example jobs and links from O*NET.
When: Early in career exploration, or next to a work-interest inventory to see whether hobbies and work interests point the same way.

Using the suite

  • You can switch between checks. Each one keeps its answers until you clear it or close this file.
  • Each check makes two separate reports: a client report and a counselor report. The counselor report stays hidden until staff open it, and each one prints on its own.
  • When you finish with a client, press New client: clear all. It empties every check at once.
  • Each check has its own Contrast, text size and Read aloud buttons. The Menu contrast button at the top changes this menu only.
  • Nothing typed here is sent anywhere or saved on this computer.
