How to use this suite
  • Choose the adult or student check that fits your current situation. Adult students may use either.
  • Switch between checks without losing answers while this file remains open. Closing or reloading clears the session.
  • Use the report to choose one or two next steps, a helper and a review date.
  • Client and counselor reports print separately. The counselor view is collapsed, not access protected.
  • Use New client: clear all when finished. Printed pages and saved PDFs need separate secure handling.
  • Answers are not uploaded or saved by this suite. O*NET opens only when you select a link; names and answers are not included. Read aloud requires a local English voice.
  • Each check has text-size and read-aloud controls. The Contrast button at the top changes the menu and every check at once.
For counselors: use with HHS / VR procedures

These informal tools support conversation, client choice and individualized planning. Self-report and career examples do not establish eligibility, observed capacity, service approval or funding. Follow agency procedures for assessment, records, confidentiality and plan development.

Review against North Dakota HHS VR operational policies and the applicable policy manual before workplace adoption. This customized suite has not been reviewed or approved by HHS.
